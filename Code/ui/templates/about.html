ContractNerd International

Our Mission

ContractNerd International is an academic AI legal-technology prototype for reviewing contracts across jurisdictions. The system helps identify missing information, ambiguity, potentially prejudicial terms, potentially unenforceable clauses, and source-backed legal issues.

The application currently supports rental agreement analysis across multiple jurisdictions, including China-based jurisdictions and India - Delhi.

Academic Foundation

This application extends the ContractNerd research pipeline for identifying unenforceable, ambiguous, and prejudicial clauses in contracts using large language models and jurisdiction-specific legal references.

Read the foundation paper here:
ContractNerd: An AI Tool to Find Unenforceable, Ambiguous, and Prejudicial Clauses in Contracts
