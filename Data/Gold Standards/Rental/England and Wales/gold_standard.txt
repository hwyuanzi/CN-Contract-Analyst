ENGLAND AND WALES RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT GOLD STANDARD

A strong residential rental agreement for England and Wales should clearly include:

1. Parties
- Full legal name of landlord
- Full legal name of tenant
- Contact addresses for notices
- Agent details, if any

2. Property
- Full address of the rented property
- Description of included rooms, fixtures, fittings, furniture, parking, and storage
- Inventory/check-in schedule where relevant

3. Term
- Start date
- End date or rolling/periodic basis
- Whether the tenancy is intended to be an assured shorthold tenancy, if applicable
- Renewal or extension process

4. Rent
- Rent amount
- Payment frequency
- Due date
- Payment method
- Late-payment consequences if legally permitted and reasonable

5. Deposit
- Deposit amount
- Tenancy deposit protection requirement, where applicable
- Deposit return timeline
- Permitted deductions
- Inventory/check-out evidence process
- Dispute process

6. Permitted payments and fees
- Rent
- Deposit
- Utilities
- Council tax
- Communication services
- TV licence where applicable
- Any default fees only where permitted and reasonable

7. Repairs and condition
- Landlord responsibility for structure, exterior, installations, sanitation, heating, water, gas, and electrical systems where applicable
- Tenant responsibility for minor upkeep and tenant-caused damage
- Procedure for reporting repairs
- Emergency repair procedure
- Fair wear and tear protection

8. Use and occupation
- Residential-use restriction
- Occupancy limits
- Subletting/assignment rules
- Guest rules if needed
- No nuisance or illegal use

9. Landlord access
- Reasonable prior notice
- Reasonable hours
- Emergency exception
- Purpose of access such as inspection or repairs

10. Termination
- Notice requirements
- Early termination process
- Consequences of breach
- Requirement to follow lawful possession procedure

11. Utilities and taxes
- Responsibility for electricity, gas, water, internet, council tax, and other services
- Meter reading process at move-in and move-out

12. Dispute resolution and jurisdiction
- Governing law
- Courts or tribunal route where appropriate
- Contact method for notices

13. Signature and execution
- Signature blocks for landlord and tenant
- Date of signing
- Witnesses or deed formalities if needed
